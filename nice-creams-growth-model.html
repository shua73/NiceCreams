The plan, phase by phase

How each period earns the right to the next.

1
Months 0–6
Gaslight Village, EGR
  • Turn Gaslight traffic into a regional following
  • Fuel demand on social — flavor drops, creator collabs, guest posts
  • Refine flavors and the counter experience
  • Launch loyalty + text list; start reading daypart data
2
Months 6–12
Gaslight Village
  • Add the breakfast yogurt daypart to wake up morning sales
  • Write the SOPs so the shop runs without the founder
  • Re-bid mix, cups & dairy to cut cost per serving
  • Use follower maps + drive-time to pick shop two
3
Year 2
Eastown
  • Open a young, walkable, brand-native market
  • Run the Gaslight playbook line for line
  • Stand up shared prep + multi-shop management
4
Year 2.5
Market Ave riverfront (Acrisure Amphitheater)
  • Enter GR's new 31-acre district — ~300k event-goers a season
  • Grab-and-go format with event-day surge staffing
  • Mobile order + district event partnerships
5
Year 3
Rockford + Ada
  • Rockford (riverwalk families) and Ada Village (affluent)
  • Bring the commissary online; hire the first area manager
  • Lock regional supply contracts
6
Years 4–5
Detroit metro & East Lansing → retail
  • Enter trend-setters: Royal Oak, Birmingham, Ferndale, East Lansing
  • Scale toward ~12 shops; weigh franchising vs. company-owned
  • Launch Pints & Treats — packaged pints in grocery
